💳 How to pay for salon services using WhaPay

📲 Method 1 – Online via Card or M-Pesa STK Push
Click “Pay Now” on any salon card. Choose Visa, Mastercard, or M-Pesa STK push. Enter amount and confirm. The salon receives instant notification and prepares your appointment.
🔢 Method 2 – Offline using Member Code (No internet)
Copy the salon’s Member Code (e.g., DK67890). Open M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Paybill → enter Member Code → amount → PIN. Works even without data. You’ll receive an SMS receipt – show as proof of payment.
📱 Method 3 – WhatsApp booking & payment link
Tap “Chat on WhatsApp”, tell them your desired service (e.g., “haircut and beard trim”), and they’ll send a secure payment link. Pay with card or M-Pesa, then show up at your appointment.

💆‍♀️ Smart tips when paying for beauty services with card or WhatsApp

  • Always confirm the price before paying – ask for a breakdown (haircut, braiding, colour, etc.).
  • For group bookings (e.g., bridal party), use one payment and split costs later.
  • Save your favourite salon’s Member Code for repeat visits – no need to ask every time.
  • Check for loyalty discounts – many salons offer a free service after 5 payments via WhaPay.
  • If you’re paying offline with Member Code, take a screenshot of the M-Pesa confirmation.
❓ Frequently asked questions about salon payments
Can I pay for a haircut using M-Pesa offline?
Yes – each salon has a Member Code. Open M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Paybill → enter the code. No internet needed.
Do these salons accept Visa and Mastercard?
Absolutely. All listed salons accept local and international Visa/Mastercard. You can also use Google Pay or Apple Pay online.
Is there a salon in Nairobi that does bridal makeup and takes cards?
Yes, James Beauty Salon (Westlands) and others offer bridal packages. Contact via WhatsApp for pricing.
Can I book a massage and pay with Member Code?
Yes – many spas accept offline Member Code payment. Confirm availability and price via WhatsApp first.
Do I need to create an account to pay?
No. You can pay as a guest using the Member Code or payment link. Registering gives you your own Member Code for personal use.
Can I pay for a friend’s salon visit from abroad?
Yes – use the “Pay Now” button with your international card. The salon receives KES instantly.
What if I don’t like the service after paying?
Discuss with the salon manager. WhaPay facilitates payment; disputes are between you and the business.
Can I pay for hair braiding using M-Pesa Paybill?
Yes – use the Member Code exactly as you would for any other Paybill payment.
Are there salons that offer men's grooming (beard trim, haircut)?
Yes – many barbershops listed accept cards and M-Pesa. Check the descriptions.
How long does it take for the salon to confirm payment?
Instantly. Both you and the salon receive an M-Pesa or card confirmation within seconds.
Can I pay for a nail extension using offline Member Code?
Absolutely – copy the code, pay, and show the confirmation SMS at your appointment.
Does WhaPay charge extra for beauty service payments?
No extra fee for customers. Merchants pay only for card transactions (1.5% + KES 20). Member Code payments have zero WhaPay fees.
Can I tip the stylist via WhaPay?
Yes – add the tip amount to your payment and mention it in the WhatsApp chat.
What if I have a coupon or discount code?
Ask the salon to adjust the invoice before you pay via payment link or Member Code.
Is my payment information kept safe?
Yes. All card transactions are PCI-DSS compliant. WhaPay never stores your card details.
