Built in Kenya.
Built for the World.

WhaPay was born from a simple observation — millions of Kenyan merchants were losing sales every day because their customers had no internet, no cards, and no way to pay digitally.

We built the world's first offline-first payment platform. Register via voice call or SMS. Accept M-Pesa without internet. Receive payments from the UK, USA and UAE via card. All from one platform, at the lowest fees in Kenya.

Our Journey

From Idea to Impact

The story of how WhaPay came to be

2025 – The Spark

Founder David Ochieng realized that millions of Kenyans had no way to accept digital payments because they lacked internet. The idea of WhaPay was born: payments that work offline.

Early 2026 – Prototype & Beta

First version launched with WhatsApp registration, QR codes, and Member Code payments. 500+ merchants joined within 3 months.

Mid 2026 – Offline Registration

Introduced voice call and SMS registration via 0140933042. Now anyone with a basic phone can get a Member Code and QR.

Today – Global Ambitions

Adding international card payments, AI fraud detection, and cross-border settlements. Preparing to expand beyond Kenya to Nigeria, Ghana, India, and Brazil. 🇰🇪 WHAPAY DIGITAL SOLUTION (BN-VZSEMLZB)
