💳 How to pay for food using WhaPay

📲 Method 1 – Online via Card or M-Pesa STK Push
Click “Pay Now” on any restaurant card. Choose Visa, Mastercard, or M-Pesa STK push. Enter amount and confirm. The restaurant receives instant notification and starts preparing your order.
🔢 Method 2 – Offline using Member Code (No internet)
Copy the restaurant’s Member Code (e.g., DK66666). Open M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Paybill → enter Member Code → amount → PIN. Works even without data. You’ll receive an SMS receipt – show it as proof.
📱 Method 3 – WhatsApp ordering & payment link
Tap “Chat on WhatsApp”, tell them what you want (e.g., “2 pizzas and drinks”), and they’ll send a secure payment link. Pay with card or M-Pesa, and your food is on its way.

🍴 Smart tips when paying for meals with WhatsApp or card

  • Always confirm your total bill before paying – including service charge and VAT.
  • For large groups, use the offline Member Code – one person pays and others reimburse via M-Pesa.
  • If ordering delivery, ask for the delivery fee and estimated time via WhatsApp first.
  • Check for daily specials or discounts – many restaurants offer them to WhaPay users.
  • Save your favourite restaurant’s Member Code for quick repeat orders.
❓ Frequently asked questions about paying for meals
Can I pay for nyama choma using M-Pesa offline?
Yes – each restaurant has a Member Code. Open M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Paybill → enter the code. No internet needed.
Do these restaurants accept Visa and Mastercard?
Absolutely. All listed restaurants accept local and international Visa/Mastercard. You can also use Google Pay or Apple Pay online.
Is there a restaurant in Nairobi that delivers and takes card?
Yes, many like Nairobi Bistro (Kilimani) offer delivery. Ask via WhatsApp.
Can I pay for a group meal with one card?
Yes – use the “Pay Now” button for the total amount. Divide the bill among friends later.
Do I need to create an account to pay?
No. You can pay as a guest using the Member Code or payment link. Registering gives you your own Member Code for personal use.
Can I order halal food and pay with M-Pesa?
Many restaurants offer halal options. Ask via WhatsApp before ordering.
What if the restaurant messes up my order after I paid?
Contact the restaurant directly. WhaPay facilitates payment; disputes are between you and the eatery.
Can I use the offline Member Code for takeaway?
Yes, very common. Call or WhatsApp your order, then pay offline with Member Code before picking up.
Does WhaPay offer refunds for cancelled orders?
Refunds are handled by the restaurant. Always confirm cancellation policy before paying.
Can I pay for a friend’s meal from abroad?
Yes – use the “Pay Now” button with your international card. The restaurant receives KES instantly.
Are there vegetarian or vegan restaurants on this directory?
Yes, several. Filter by menu – contact via WhatsApp to confirm options.
How do I know if a restaurant delivers to my area?
Send a WhatsApp message with your location. Most will tell you delivery fee and ETA.
What is the cheapest restaurant that accepts card payments?
Prices vary. Eldoret City Diner offers budget meals from KES 150 – cards accepted.
Can I reserve a table and pay later via Member Code?
Yes, many restaurants accept advance payment via Member Code to secure your booking.
How long does it take for the restaurant to confirm payment?
Instantly. Both you and the restaurant receive an M-Pesa or card confirmation within seconds.
