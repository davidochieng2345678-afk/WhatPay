Frequently Asked Questions – Pricing & Billing

❓ Is registration really free?

Yes. Registration via WhatsApp, voice call (0140933042), or SMS is completely free. You only pay when you receive a payment.

❓ Are there any monthly or daily fees?

No. WhaPay has zero monthly fees and zero daily fees. You only pay per successful transaction.

❓ How much does M-Pesa cost?

WhaPay does not add any fee on M-Pesa. You pay only the standard Safaricom rates (e.g., 1% + KES 10 for Paybill).

❓ What about card payments?

1.5% + KES 20 per successful transaction. This covers Stripe's fees plus WhaPay's service fee.

❓ Do customers pay any fees?

No. Customers never pay any fees to use WhaPay. Registration, sending payments, scanning QR codes – all free.

❓ Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. You only pay for transactions you actually processed.

WhaPay – Global Payment Gateway with Africa's Lowest Transaction Fees

WhaPay is a global payment platform built for merchants in Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Nigeria, Ghana and beyond, while also accepting payments from customers in the USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, India and Saudi Arabia. Whether your customer is paying from Nairobi or New York, WhaPay processes it in seconds.

For Kenyan merchants, WhaPay offers the lowest card transaction fee in the market — just 1.5% + KES 20 (approximately $0.15 USD) per successful transaction. There are zero monthly fees, zero daily fees and zero setup costs. M-Pesa transactions carry no WhaPay platform fee — you only pay Safaricom's standard Paybill rate.

For international merchants and diaspora payments, WhaPay accepts 135+ currencies including USD, GBP, EUR, AED, INR, CAD, AUD, SAR, CNY, JPY, NGN and ZAR. International customers can pay using Visa, Mastercard, Google Pay, Apple Pay, Samsung Pay and PayPal. The merchant receives the equivalent amount in KES or their local currency.

WhaPay is the only payment gateway in Africa that offers both online and offline payment acceptance. Merchants in rural Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania or anywhere with poor internet connectivity can still register via voice call or SMS to 0140933042 and accept payments using the offline Member Code + M-Pesa Paybill system — no smartphone or internet required.

Compared to other African payment gateways, WhaPay offers significantly lower fees. Pesapal charges 2.9% + KES 20 per card transaction. Paystack charges 2.9% + KES 30. IntaSend charges 2.5% + KES 20. WhaPay charges just 1.5% + KES 20 — saving merchants thousands of shillings every month on high-volume transactions.
