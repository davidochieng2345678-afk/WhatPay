❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register without internet?

Dial 0140933042 and follow voice prompts, or send SMS "REG YourName" to 0140933042.

What are the fees?

M-Pesa: 0% fee. Cards: 1.5% + KES 20. Airtel/MTN/Tigo/Orange: 1.5% + KES 10. No monthly fees.

How do I pay offline?

Open M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Paybill 0140933042 → Account = Member Code → Amount → PIN.

Can I receive payments from abroad?

Yes! Anyone with a card can pay your Member Code from anywhere in the world.

How do I get my QR code?

After registration, use the QR Code Generator in this page to create and download your QR code.

Is my money safe?

WhaPay never holds your money. Payments go directly to your M-Pesa or bank account.

What is a Member Code?

A unique code (e.g., DK12345) that identifies you on WhaPay. Use it to receive payments – even offline.

Do I need a bank account?

No. You can receive payments directly to your M-Pesa account. Bank accounts are optional for larger transfers.
