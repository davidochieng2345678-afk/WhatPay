❓ Frequently asked questions about grocery shopping
Can I pay for groceries offline using M-Pesa?
Yes! Each store has a unique Member Code (e.g., DK12345). Open M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Paybill → enter Member Code → amount → PIN. No internet needed.
Do these grocery stores accept international cards?
Absolutely. All listed merchants accept local and international Visa/Mastercard. You can also use Google Pay or Apple Pay where supported.
Do they deliver groceries nationwide?
Most stores offer delivery – contact them via WhatsApp or phone to confirm delivery fees and timelines.
Is my payment secure?
WhaPay never holds your money. Payments go directly to the merchant via M-Pesa or card processor. All transactions are SSL encrypted.
