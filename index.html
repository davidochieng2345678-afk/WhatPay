Offline Payment Guide (No Internet)

Even without internet, you can accept payments via M-PESA using your member code. Sync later.

1. Share your Member Code (e.g., DK10245)
2. Customer pays via M-PESA Paybill – Account = Your Code
3. Keep SMS as proof
4. When online, send SYNC on WhatsApp to update balance.
📲 Printed QR
Display your QR code. Customer scans later (or uses details) to pay. Works offline too.

M-PESA works without internet – always rely on SMS confirmation.
