Use WhaPay Even Without Internet

Register, pay, and complete transactions anytime — even offline.

Offline Registration

Register without internet – via SMS or WhatsApp.

  • SMS: Send REG Your Name to 0140933042
  • WhatsApp: Send "REGISTER" to 0140933042
  • You receive a Temporary Code → activate via WhatsApp
  • Get permanent Member Code + QR

Offline Payment (Code)

Pay using merchant's code – no internet required.

  • Customer gets merchant code (e.g., DK10245)
  • M-PESA → Lipa na M-PESA → Paybill/Till
  • Enter Account = Merchant Code, amount, confirm
  • Both receive SMS – keep as proof
  • When online: send SYNC via WhatsApp to 0140933042

Printed QR Code

Display your QR – customers scan and pay, even offline.

  • Print your unique QR (sent after registration)
  • Customer scans → sees merchant code + Till
  • Opens M-PESA manually (no internet)
  • Pays using the details shown
  • SMS receipt saved; sync later via WhatsApp

💡 Why Offline Matters

With WhaPay, you are not limited by internet connection. Register via voice call or SMS to 0140933042, pay using M-PESA's SMS network, and sync automatically when back online.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions by Kenyan Merchants

How do I register my business to accept M-Pesa payments without internet in Kenya?

Three ways — no internet needed. Dial 0140933042 and follow the voice prompts, send REG YourBusinessName via SMS to the same number, or send REGISTER on WhatsApp. You receive your Merchant Code and QR instantly via SMS.

How much does it cost for a Kenyan small business to accept card payments?

Only 1.5% + KES 20 per successful card transaction. No monthly fees, no setup fees. M-Pesa payments have zero extra platform fees. The lowest-cost payment gateway for small businesses in Kenya.

Can my customers pay me via M-Pesa Paybill when they have no internet?

Yes. Customers open M-PESA → Lipa na M-PESA → Paybill → enter your Member Code as account number → enter amount → PIN. No internet needed by either party. Both receive SMS confirmation instantly.

How do I send a WhatsApp payment link to customers in Kenya?

Use the Payment Link Generator in your dashboard. Enter your Member Code and amount, click Create Link, and share via WhatsApp. Customers click the link and pay via M-Pesa STK push or card in seconds — no app download needed.

Does WhaPay work for street vendors and small shops in rural Kenya without smartphones?

Yes. WhaPay was built for low-connectivity areas. Register via voice call or basic SMS, accept payments via M-Pesa Paybill using your offline Member Code, print your QR to display at your counter, and sync records when internet is available.
