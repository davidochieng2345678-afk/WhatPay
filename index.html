Why Member Codes are revolutionary:

  • Each merchant gets a unique code (e.g., DK12345).
  • Customers pay by entering this code as the Account Number in M-Pesa Paybill.
  • No internet required – uses M-Pesa's USSD network.
  • Money goes directly from customer to merchant – WhaPay never touches funds.
  • Merchants get instant SMS confirmation; receipts are also sent via WhatsApp when online.
This is the only offline payment method that works without any data connection.

❓ Pricing FAQs

Are there any hidden fees?

No. WhaPay charges only the transaction fee (1.5% + KES 20 on card) and the monthly plan fee (if you choose Business). M‑Pesa payments incur only Safaricom’s standard charges – we add nothing.

Do I pay for offline registration?

No. Registration via voice call or SMS to 0140933042 is completely free. You only pay when you receive card payments.

Can I downgrade or cancel my Business Plan?

Yes. You can cancel or downgrade anytime via your dashboard or by contacting us on WhatsApp. No penalties.

How are fees settled?

Fees are deducted automatically from the transaction amount before payout to your M‑Pesa or bank account. You receive the net amount instantly. No invoices, no manual payments.

How Offline Sync Works

1

Store Offline

Registrations and payments are saved locally using IndexedDB (via localForage) when you're offline.

2

Auto-Sync

When you reconnect, items are automatically sent to the server for processing.

3

Retry Logic

If a sync fails, we automatically retry with exponential backoff (up to 5 attempts).

4

Conflict Resolution

Server wins by default. You can view and retry failed items manually.
