Powerful Features for Kenyan Merchants

Accept M-Pesa & card payments, scan QR codes, track transactions live, generate detailed reports, and accept payments even without internet. WhaPay is built for real‑world Kenyan businesses.

WhatsApp Payment Links

Generate instant payment links directly in WhatsApp chats. Customers pay with M-Pesa or card without leaving the conversation – no app switching, no friction.

✔ 2‑click checkout

M-Pesa STK Push

Seamless M-Pesa integration via Daraja API. Customers receive a payment prompt on their phone – they enter PIN and pay instantly. No manual reference numbers.

✔ Real‑time confirmation

Card Payments (Visa/Mastercard)

Accept credit and debit cards via Stripe. Ideal for customers who prefer cards over mobile money. Works for local and international cards.

✔ Secure & PCI compliant

QR & Barcode Payments

Each merchant gets a unique QR code. Customers scan with their phone – amount is pre‑filled, payment is instant. Perfect for retail counters and takeaways.

✔ Zero typing errors

Live Dashboard

Real‑time overview of total revenue, pending payments, weekly/monthly trends, and top customers. Know your business health at a glance.

✔ Exportable reports

Automated Reports

Weekly and monthly reports sent directly to your WhatsApp. See top‑paying customers, most used payment methods, and revenue growth.

✔ Actionable insights

Offline Payments

No internet? No problem. Customers pay using your merchant code via M-PESA Paybill/Till. SMS receipts are saved and synced automatically when back online.

✔ Works in low‑network areas

WhatsApp Bot Commands

Customers can pay by sending simple text commands like PAY DK10245 500. Also check balance, view history, and register – all via WhatsApp.

✔ No app needed

24/7 WhatsApp Support

Direct support line to our team. Get help with registration, payments, or technical issues within minutes – not days.

✔ Real human assistance
