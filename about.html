Our Mission

To democratize payments by providing a single platform that works online (WhatsApp) and offline (voice/SMS), empowering businesses and individuals everywhere – even without internet. **We believe connectivity should never be a barrier to commerce.**

Our Vision

To become the world's most inclusive payment network – **connecting 1 billion users** through WhatsApp, voice, SMS, and M-Pesa, while leading in **AI fraud prevention, unified commerce, and cross-border settlements**. From Kenya to the world.

Our Journey – From Idea to Impact

2025 – The Spark

Founder David Ochieng realized that millions of Kenyans had no way to accept digital payments because they lacked internet. **The idea of WhaPay was born: payments that work offline.**

Early 2026 – Prototype & Beta

First version launched with WhatsApp registration, QR codes, and Member Code payments. **500+ merchants joined within 3 months.**

Mid 2026 – Offline Registration

Introduced **voice call and SMS registration** via 0140933042. Now anyone with a basic phone can get a Member Code and QR.

Today – Global Ambitions

Adding **international card payments, AI fraud detection, and cross-border settlements**. Preparing to expand beyond Kenya to Nigeria, Ghana, India, and Brazil.
