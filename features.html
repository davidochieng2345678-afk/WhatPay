WhaPay Features

Powerful WhatsApp payment solutions for Kenyan merchants. Accept M-Pesa & card payments, scan QR codes, track transactions live, and generate detailed weekly and monthly reports with insights to grow your business.

WhatsApp Payments

Generate payment links instantly within WhatsApp. Customers pay securely using M-Pesa or cards without leaving the chat, ensuring a seamless experience.

M-Pesa Integration

Seamlessly accept M-Pesa payments via STK Push. Track successful and pending transactions in real-time from your merchant dashboard with complete transparency.

Credit & Debit Cards

Enable card payments through secure Stripe integration. Accept Visa, Mastercard, and local debit cards safely, allowing more customers to pay conveniently.

QR & Barcode Scanning

Optional QR/barcode scanning for fast in-store payments. Reduce errors, speed up transactions, and improve the overall customer experience.

Live Dashboard

Monitor all payments in real-time. View totals, pending transactions, weekly and monthly summaries, and trend analysis to make informed business decisions.

Detailed Reports

Automated weekly and monthly reports. Identify top-paying customers, payment methods, revenue trends, and get actionable insights to optimize your sales.

Merchant Friendly

Designed specifically for small and medium Kenyan businesses. Enjoy a simple interface, quick onboarding, and secure payment processing for peace of mind.

Secure & Compliant

All transactions are fully encrypted and compliant with local regulations. Protect both merchants and customers with top-tier security standards.

WhatsApp Chat Support

Direct WhatsApp support accessible from your dashboard. Quickly resolve issues and respond to customer queries with ease.
