🚀 How to Register, Get Your QR Code & Receive Payments

Welcome! This guide will help you register, get your unique code/QR, and start receiving payments easily using your phone — both online and offline.

📲 Step 1: Register on WhatsApp

  • Open WhatsApp
  • Send this message to our business number: REGISTER
  • You will be asked to provide: Your Full Name and Your Phone Number
WhatsApp: +254 710 346425

🆔 Step 2: Get Your Member Code & QR Code

✅ Registration Successful!
Name: [Your Name]
Member Code: DK10245
👉 You will also receive your QR Code

Your Member Code is your personal account number. Your QR Code is your shortcut for payments.

💳 Step 3: How Customers Pay You

✅ Option 1: Using Your Member Code

Customer sends on WhatsApp:

PAY DK10245 500

Meaning: Pay KES 500 to account DK10245

✅ Option 2: Using Your QR Code (Recommended)

  • Customer scans your QR code
  • A payment page opens automatically
  • They enter amount and confirm

✔ Faster • Easier • No typing errors

💸 Step 4: Payment Method (M-PESA)

All payments are made through M-PESA Paybill or Till Number.

Customer will:
1. Go to M-PESA → Lipa na M-PESA → Paybill/Till
2. Enter Business Number
3. Enter Account Number = Your Member Code (e.g., DK10245)
4. Enter Amount → PIN → Confirm

✅ Step 5: Instant Confirmation

✅ Payment Received
Amount: KES 500
New Balance: KES 1,200

✔ You can track all your transactions easily from your dashboard.

📡 Offline Payment Option (No Internet Needed)

We understand that sometimes both the merchant and customer may not have internet access. Don’t worry — you can still complete transactions and sync later.

🔌 How Offline Payment Works

Even without internet, you can: Register customers • Record transactions • Accept payments via M-PESA • Sync automatically once back online.

📲 Option 1: Offline Code Payment (Recommended)

  1. Share Your Member Code (e.g., DK10245)
  2. Customer pays via M-PESA – Lipa na M-PESA → Paybill/Till → Account Number = Your Member Code
  3. Keep SMS Confirmation – Both parties receive M-PESA SMS as proof
  4. Record transaction manually (optional): write down amount + M-PESA code
  5. Sync when back online – Open WhatsApp, send SYNC or system auto-updates

Example record: DK10245 | KES 500 | QHG7X2ABC

🔁 Option 2: Offline QR (Printed QR Code)

Merchant shows printed QR code. Customer scans later when online, or uses QR details manually. QR contains Member Code + payment instructions.

Important: M-PESA works without internet (uses SIM network). Always rely on SMS confirmation as proof. Sync as soon as internet is available.

Register without internet – via SMS or WhatsApp.
