🇰🇪 Why WhaPay is the #1 payment choice for grocery shoppers in Kenya

Unlike traditional payment gateways that require stable internet and often freeze merchant funds, WhaPay was built for the reality of Kenyan commerce. Our Member Code system lets you pay for groceries even when your phone has zero data. You just open M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Paybill → enter the store’s Member Code → amount → PIN. It’s instant, secure, and leaves a digital receipt.

Moreover, every grocery store in our directory accepts Visa and Mastercard – so you can use your salary card, travel card, or any international card without worries. Combined with WhatsApp ordering and live chat, you get a seamless shopping experience from product selection to payment.

💳 How to pay for groceries using WhaPay (3 simple methods)

📲 Method 1 – Online via Card or M-Pesa STK Push
Click the “Pay Now” button on any merchant card. You’ll be redirected to our secure payment page. Choose Visa, Mastercard, or M-Pesa STK push. Enter amount and confirm. Money goes directly to the merchant – WhaPay never holds funds.
🔢 Method 2 – Offline using Member Code (No internet)
Copy the Member Code shown on the merchant card (e.g., DK12345). Open M-Pesa → Lipa na M-Pesa → Paybill → Enter Business Number (the Member Code) → Account (optional, can be your name) → Amount → PIN. Both you and the merchant receive SMS confirmation. Works anywhere, anytime.
📱 Method 3 – WhatsApp ordering & payment link
Tap the “Chat on WhatsApp” button. Tell the store what you need. They will send you a secure WhaPay payment link. Click it, pay with card or M-Pesa, and they’ll prepare your order.

🛍️ Useful tips when paying for groceries with a card in Kenya

  • Always double-check the Member Code before sending offline M-Pesa payments – a wrong code means money lost.
  • For large orders (over KES 10,000), card payment is often faster and comes with better consumer protection.
  • If you are a regular customer, ask the store to save your Member Code for quick offline payments.
  • Use the WhatsApp chat to confirm stock availability and prices before paying – avoid disappointment.
  • Share your receipt (M-Pesa SMS) with the store as proof of payment.
  • Look for stores with high ratings and many reviews – they are more reliable.
