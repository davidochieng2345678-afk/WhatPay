👆 How WhaPay Works – Simple Steps

1️⃣

Get Your Code & QR

Register online (WhatsApp) or offline (voice/SMS to 0140933042). Receive your unique Member Code and QR code instantly.

2️⃣

Share or Scan

Merchant shares their WhatsApp link, QR code, or Member Code. Customer scans or copies the code.

3️⃣

Pay & Confirm

Pay via M-Pesa or card (online) or Member Code + M-Pesa Paybill 0140933042 (offline). Both receive instant confirmation.
