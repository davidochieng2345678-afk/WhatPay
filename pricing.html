Frequently Asked Questions – Pricing & Billing

❓ Is registration really free?

Yes. Registration via WhatsApp, voice call (0140933042), or SMS is completely free. You only pay when you receive a payment.

❓ Are there any monthly or daily fees?

No. WhaPay has zero monthly fees and zero daily fees. You only pay per successful transaction.

❓ How much does M-Pesa cost?

WhaPay does not add any fee on M-Pesa. You pay only the standard Safaricom rates (e.g., 1% + KES 10 for Paybill).

❓ What about card payments?

1.5% + KES 20 per successful transaction. This covers Stripe's fees plus WhaPay's service fee.

❓ Do customers pay any fees?

No. Customers never pay any fees to use WhaPay. Registration, sending payments, scanning QR codes – all free.

❓ Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts, no cancellation fees. You only pay for transactions you actually processed.
